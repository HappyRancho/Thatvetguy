Dr. Amaan Ahmed brings a rare combination of multi-species clinical expertise, wildlife rehabilitation fieldwork, and animal welfare advocacy to the Collective. A 2025 BVSc graduate of the College of Veterinary Science & A.H., Mhow, his training spans zoo medicine, equine care, avian biosecurity, diagnostic pathology, and high-volume emergency outpatient practice. His voluntary contributions to wildlife research and conservation campaigns — partnered with WWF, UNICEF, and Chhattisgarh government bodies — give him a distinctly One Health perspective that informs every piece of content he authors.
