Project Overview

Securetrack, a leading technology firm based in Riyadh, KSA, required a dedicated content strategy to elevate their brand authority in the security and technology sector. The ThatVetGuy Collective was contracted to provide high-level, strictly confidential ghostwriting services tailored to their corporate audience.

Scope of Deliverables

The engagement focuses on maintaining a robust and consistent communication pipeline, translating complex technological and security paradigms into digestible, engaging thought leadership.

  • Weekly Newsletters: Drafting and finalizing 4 comprehensive newsletters per month focused on industry trends, security infrastructure, and brand updates.
  • Content Calendar Management: Strategizing and finalizing four core topics at the beginning of each month to ensure alignment with the client's marketing objectives.
  • Long-Form Content: Producing in-depth blog articles and B2B assets published under the Securetrack brand.

Confidentiality & Ghostwriting

Operating under a strict freelance ghostwriting agreement, our collective produces all content with full intellectual property transfer upon completion. This ensures the Securetrack brand maintains exclusive ownership and seamless brand voice consistency across all published platforms, without visible third-party attribution.
