Indore Kennel

A strictly managed, professional environment functioning as our primary physical infrastructure for real-world canine evaluations, sample testing, and controlled behavioral observation.

Facility & Operational Overview

Indore Kennel operates as the designated, on-the-ground facility for The ThatVetGuy Collective. Unlike standard boarding environments, it is engineered to support professional corporate requirements, providing an ethical, highly controlled setting for pet-care brands conducting product evaluations, nutritional sample testing, and long-term behavioral studies.

Spanning multiple specialized branches, the facility strictly adheres to clinical safety guidelines, ensuring data integrity for B2B stakeholders while maintaining a stress-free, enrichment-focused retreat for its primary canine residents.

Leadership & Management

Fear Free Certified Veterinarian | Facility Owner

Dr. Nigam has successfully owned and managed 3 distinct branches of the Indore Kennel pet hostel network over the past 5 years.
